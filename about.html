About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was founded with a passion for traditional neighborhood baking. We believe that good bread takes time, care, and quality ingredients. From our early morning bake cycles to our welcoming storefront, we are committed to serving our local community with warmth and authenticity.

Behind the Scenes in Our Kitchen

Take a look inside our baking process as fresh sourdough loaves come hot out of the deck oven!

Sourcing Philosophy

We collaborate directly with regional farmers to source 100% organic, non-GMO stone-ground flour and whole grains. Our dairy comes from local family-owned farms, ensuring that every pastry and loaf supports sustainable regional agriculture.

Staff Highlights

Elena Rostova - Master Baker & Founder

Elena spent over fifteen years perfecting European fermentation techniques before opening North Star Bakery to bring authentic sourdough back to the community.

Marcus Vance - Head Pastry Chef

Marcus specializes in classic viennoiserie, bringing precision and artistic flair to every croissant and seasonal tart created in our kitchen.
